#### Detailed Notes

##### Overview of Word Embeddings
- **Definition**: Numerical representations of words as dense vectors, capturing semantic relationships based on context.
- **Importance**: Compactness, reusability, and the ability to capture meaning.

##### Embedding Modalities
- Representations can extend beyond text to images, audio, video, code, and biological sequences.

##### Word2Vec Training Approaches
- **Skip-gram**: Predicts context words from a center word.
- **CBOW**: Predicts a center word from context words.

##### Visualization and Analysis
- Word embeddings can be visualized in 2D or 3D to interpret relationships. Vector arithmetic can reveal analogies (e.g., `woman - man + king ≈ queen`).

##### Neural Network Structure
- **Input Layer**: One-hot vector representation of the center word.
- **Hidden Layer**: Contains the weight matrix representing embeddings.
- **Output Layer**: Produces a probability distribution over the vocabulary.

##### Downsampling Frequent Words
- Common words are downsampled during training to prevent them from dominating the learning process.

#### Examples & Applications
- **Search Engines**: Match queries to semantically similar documents.
- **Recommendation Systems**: Represent items as vectors for personalized recommendations.
- **Chatbots**: Map user inputs to intents.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Classify text sentiment based on embedding similarity.
- **Fraud Detection**: Identify unusual transaction patterns.

#### Key Takeaways
- Word embeddings are essential for modern NLP tasks, enabling models to understand and process language effectively.
- They provide a foundation for various applications in AI, including sentiment analysis, machine translation, and more.
- Understanding the structure and training of Word2Vec is crucial for leveraging embeddings in practical applications.
