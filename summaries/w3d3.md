## Detailed Notes

### Project Structure
- **Duration**: The project spans several days, culminating in presentations.
- **Collaboration**: Students are encouraged to work in pairs or small groups, sharing ideas and strategies.

- **CIFAR-10**: 10 classes, 32x32 pixel images.

### Data Management
- **Storage**: Save datasets on Google Drive; manage space effectively by deleting unnecessary files.

### Model Development
- **Architecture**: Build CNNs with convolutional, pooling, fully connected, and softmax layers.
- **Training**: Train multiple models with different architectures and hyperparameters.
- **Regularization**: Use techniques like Dropout to prevent overfitting.

### Evaluation Metrics
- **Accuracy**: Common metric but should be supplemented with others.
- **Confusion Matrix**: Provides insights into model performance.
- **Precision and Recall**: Important for nuanced evaluation.

### Hyperparameter Tuning
- Change one or two parameters at a time to isolate effects.
- Experiment with network complexity and optimization strategies.

### Communication and Support
- **Help Requests**: Use designated channels for assistance.
- **Squad Meetings**: Hold regular meetings for progress updates.

### Project Management Methodologies
- **Agile and Kanban**: Apply these methodologies for project planning and execution.
- **Stand-up Meetings**: Regular check-ins to discuss progress.
